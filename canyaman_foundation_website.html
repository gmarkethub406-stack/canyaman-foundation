Who we are

A commitment to children and their futures.

CANYAMAN Foundation is a proposed children-focused charitable initiative committed to supporting vulnerable and underprivileged children through education, essential care and community support.

Our aim is to bring individuals, businesses, community groups and organisations together around practical work that puts children's wellbeing first.

Our initiative

The CAN Project

A fundraising and partnership initiative intended to mobilise resources for child-focused programmes and community impact through 2029.

Partnership & fundraising

We welcome enquiries from individuals, companies, philanthropists and organisations interested in supporting the CAN Project.

Proposed partnership contribution levels begin at US$500. Contributions and sponsorships will be subject to written terms, documented use of funds and appropriate reporting.

CAN Supporter$500
Bronze Partner$1,000
Silver Partner$2,500
Gold Partner$5,000
Platinum Partner$10,000+
These are proposed contribution levels, not investment products. Donations do not create an entitlement to financial returns. Any sponsorship benefits must be agreed in writing before funds are accepted.
Trust & accountability

Responsible support. Clear reporting.

We believe trust is earned through honest communication, careful stewardship and evidence of what has been delivered.

Before fundraising begins, the Foundation should publish its legal status, responsible trustees or directors, approved project budget, safeguarding policy and donation-handling process. Partners should receive appropriate updates and a final project report.

2026–2027 · Preparation
Governance, project design, partner engagement and fundraising readiness.
2028 · Implementation
Approved activities, delivery monitoring and progress updates.
2029 · Review & reporting
Target project close-out, impact review and final accountability report.
